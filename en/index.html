Frequently asked questions

How do I extract text from an image?

Open the app, choose From image, pick the picture, and the text appears so you can copy or share it. Arabic and English are supported.

Can I convert a video or audio file to text?

Yes. Choose From video or From audio and upload the file. The text is produced by AI and needs internet, so double-check it before relying on it.

Does it handle Arabic and Egyptian dialect?

It tries to write the speech as spoken in Arabic script. Accuracy depends on audio quality and varies between recordings.

Can I download videos from the internet?

Yes, paste the video link in the Download tab. Not every site or video works, private videos cannot be downloaded, and a logo burned into the video cannot be removed.

Is the app free?

Yes, completely free, with no subscription or payment.

Is it on Google Play?

No. You download it directly as an APK from the button above, and updates install over the old version.
