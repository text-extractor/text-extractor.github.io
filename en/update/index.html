More accurate text from images (OCR)

Our main focus: clearer, more accurate Arabic and English text from photos, screenshots and printed pages, tested on many samples before release.

Camera scan

Open the camera inside the app and snap a page or book. The text appears right away, no need to save the photo first.

3

Send images and videos in from any app

From your gallery, WhatsApp or any app: tap Share, pick Text Extractor, and extraction starts straight away.

4

Export as TXT, SRT and PDF

Save the text as a TXT or PDF file. For video and audio, save subtitles as an SRT file with timings. Share in one tap.

5

Translate the text

Translate extracted text between Arabic, English and other languages on the same screen.

6

Text from video and audio

The same speech-to-text for videos and recordings, with better speed and stability.

7

Background extraction with a notification

If you leave the app while extraction runs, it keeps going. You see progress in a status-bar notification you can tap to come back, and it tells you when it is done or failed. (Some phones may need battery set to unrestricted.)

8

Update banner and new-version alerts

The app tells you when a new version is out and you can update in one tap, no need to visit the site.

9

Email-verified accounts

When you sign up you get a verification link by email. You can use the app before verifying, but archive sync across your phones works after you verify.

Video downloader removed

The app is now fully focused on extracting text from images, video and audio.
